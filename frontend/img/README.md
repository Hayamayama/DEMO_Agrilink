# Ask AI demo photos

Ask AI → Options → Photo offers two `DEMO SAMPLE` photos for handsets without a
camera/file picker (and as a safe fallback during the judged demo). Drop real
photos here with exactly these names:

- `sample-leaf-1.jpg` — one yellowing rice leaf, daylight, fills the frame
- `sample-leaf-2.jpg` — one leaf with visible spots

Keep each under 700 KB (≈1024px long edge, JPEG). Until they exist the Photo
screen shows "Demo sample missing on server." Answers from these photos are
flagged `SAMPLE DATA`.
